# NEXUS Hackathon Demo

## Problem

Crypto dashboards show numbers, but they do not always explain what matters.

NEXUS turns market and portfolio data into an explainable CFO-style decision.

## Flow

Market Data
→ Risk Engine
→ Decision Engine
→ CFO Brief
→ Workflow Action
